# HAZ Software — Demo Guide

A point-of-sale and back-office system for car-care and retail businesses in Mauritius.
You are looking at **HAZ Demo Auto Care**, a sample company with invented data. Nothing here is real,
so click anything you like.

**Sign in:** https://app.hazsoftware.com — email and password are sent to you separately.

## A suggested 10-minute tour

1. **Customers and vehicles** — every customer has their cars (plate, make, model). Open a fleet
   customer such as *Coastal Rentals Ltd* to see several vehicles under one account.
2. **Quotes** — open a quotation, see how it is priced with 15% VAT, and send it by email or WhatsApp
   (in this demo, sends are redirected to the demo owner, never to a real customer).
3. **Quote to job** — an accepted quote with a service on it becomes a job on the job board.
4. **Invoices and payments** — invoices are numbered automatically with no gaps (`DINV-0001`…).
   Take a payment by cash, card, Juice, bank transfer, cheque or loyalty points, or part-pay and leave a balance.
5. **Stock** — products have a Shop and a Warehouse count. Every sale reduces stock and every change is
   recorded, so you can see where any number came from. Low-stock items are flagged.
6. **Sales journal and reports** — sales grouped by payment method, with the bills under each. Reports
   export to CSV for your accountant.
7. **Documents** — every quote and invoice prints as a clean A4 PDF with the VAT breakdown.

## What the system does

| Area | Highlights |
|---|---|
| Selling | Quotes, invoices, credit notes; VAT-exclusive or VAT-inclusive pricing; discounts with limits |
| Services | Job board from quotes, technicians, vehicle history |
| Stock | Multiple locations, opening stock, transfers, low-stock alerts |
| Money | Cash, card, Juice, bank, cheque, loyalty points; part-payments; till sessions and daily Z-reports |
| Customers | Accounts, fleets, loyalty points, WhatsApp and email documents |
| Control | Owner / manager / cashier roles; invoices lock once issued; full audit trail |
| Tablet till | A companion Android tablet point of sale that keeps selling without internet and syncs when it reconnects |

*The tablet app is shown separately on request.*

## Good to know

- Prices in this demo exclude 15% VAT.
- Figures are sample data created today, so reports show a single day of trading.
